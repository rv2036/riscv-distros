Bottom line

Security-focused and minimal; the experience is most orthodox on an Unmatched, otherwise not a beginner's pick.

Pitfalls

  • The main target is still the discontinued HiFive Unmatched; newer boards depend on the community
  • VisionFive 2 needs firmware config changes before it boots
  • PolarFire Icicle Kit support is incomplete
  • Graphics/wireless support on RISC-V is very limited — don't count on a desktop
