Bottom line

Ubuntu has the most detailed board support: it publishes preinstalled images per board, but 26.04 raised the bar to RVA23, leaving older boards stuck on 24.04 LTS.

Based on
Debian
Upstream
Canonical builds and hosts the riscv64 images itself ("built and hosted by Canonical"); riscv64 is an officially supported Ubuntu architecture. From 26.04 LTS the ISA baseline moves to RVA23S64.

Pitfalls

  • 26.04 LTS requires RVA23S64: non-RVA23 hardware (VisionFive 2/JH7110, SiFive Unmatched, etc.) is limited to 24.04.5 LTS — "Hardware that is not RVA23-ready continues to be supported by our 24.04.5 LTS release"
  • Some board images are headless only (no GPU, no WiFi); the official page says so explicitly under the DeepComputing FML13V01 entry
  • Board images are distinguished by a +<board> suffix (+unmatched/+jh7110/+icicle/+nezha/+licheerv/+pic64gx); the wrong one won't boot
