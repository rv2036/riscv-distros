Bottom line

FVF is a RISC-V image rebuilt from Fedora sources by a separate team, not an official Fedora release. It covers more boards than the official SIG images, so it is the practical pick when you want a Fedora userland that boots out of the box.

Based on
Fedora
Upstream
Fedora-V Force is an independent team/community project that compiles its own riscv64 builds from Fedora 36 through rawhide and adapts them to boards; its site says it is "independently compiling releases from Fedora 36 to rawhide", and it has no affiliation with Fedora's official release process.

Pitfalls

  • Images are built independently by the community, not through Fedora's signing/release process, and versions drift from upstream Fedora
  • The mirror is a JS single-page app, so scripts can't scrape the board list; pick your board by hand on the page
