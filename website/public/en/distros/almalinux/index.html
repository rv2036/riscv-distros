Bottom line

The first downstream distribution in the RHEL family to publish official riscv64 builds; without a board at hand, the GenericCloud image under QEMU lets you try it right away.

vendor
AlmaLinux OS Foundation
Based on
CentOS Stream 10 (RHEL 10 code base)
Upstream
AlmaLinux publishes official riscv64 builds (Kitten 10 is a downstream preview of CentOS Stream 10, announced in the official blog on 2026-03-17). It is an official AlmaLinux release, but not a stable product line.
Versions
Kitten 10 (riscv64; Docker images and GenericCloud VM images are already available, while board images for VF2 / P550 are described officially as "coming soon")

Boards

  • QEMU virt (virtual machine) Official The GenericCloud VM image boots directly in QEMU, as the official blog states explicitly
  • StarFive VisionFive 2 Partial Officially described as "coming soon"; no public download as of the check date
  • SiFive HiFive Premier P550 Partial Officially described as "coming soon"; no public download as of the check date

Pitfalls

  • Kitten is a downstream preview of CentOS Stream, meant for previewing where RHEL 10 is heading; it is not suitable as a stable system
  • Board images (VisionFive 2 / P550) are only described as "coming soon"; what can actually be downloaded now is the Docker image and the GenericCloud image for QEMU
  • There is no separate 10-kitten directory on the mirrors, so do not try to compose paths such as /almalinux/10-kitten/ — they return 404
